The Manifesto

A small set of beliefs.

“A tool that needs to phone home to do its job is not a tool. It is a service with a hold on your data.

We make small utilities that run on your machine. They have no servers, no telemetry, no business model predicated on keeping you. The code is plain. The dependencies are few. The file never leaves.

If we ever ship something that needs the network, we'll say so on the page that asks for the file. Until then, assume the answer is no.

— Ahmad & the team
