WHY DOES THIS EXIST?

01.

YOUR PHOTOS ARE YOURS.

Every other converter uploads your file to a server in a country you can't pronounce. Not us. We don't have a server.

02.

NO ACCOUNTS, NO LIMITS.

Convert 1 image or 1,000. We don't count. We don't even know. Open the tab, do the thing, close the tab. That's it.

03.

WORKS ON A PLANE.

Once you've loaded the app, it works without the internet. Convert files in a basement, a bunker, or a basement-bunker.

FAQ.

Is it really free? +

Yes. No ads, no upsells, no premium tier. The whole thing is a few hundred kilobytes of WebAssembly. The bandwidth cost is on us.

Where do my files go? +

Nowhere. They live in your browser's memory until you close the tab, and then they're gone. We literally cannot see them.

Why does it look like a 1995 zine? +

Because we like 1995 zines. And because brutalism is honest: thick borders and flat colour don't lie to you about what something is.
